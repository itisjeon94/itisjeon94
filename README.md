**Story-driven adventure · Released on Steam**  
Jul 2025–Sep 2026 · Team of 12 · Game Design / Unity Client Development / Field Systems Lead

A story-driven adventure about running a teahouse in the afterlife for 49 days. Players gather ingredients and build relationships during the day, then serve tea to spirits at night. Relationships and choices shape the ending.

- Designed quests, achievements, NPC dialogue, and story content.
- Implemented gathering, crop unlocks, respawning, NPC dialogue and affinity, quests and rewards, and shop systems.
- Created area unlock and fog-clearing effects, and integrated Steam achievements.
- **Highlights:** Released on Steam; exhibited in the NDM 2026 Challenge category; reached 302% of the crowdfunding goal on Tumblbug, raising approximately KRW 7.55 million from 330 backers.

`Unity (URP)` `C#` `Yarn Spinner` `Spine` `DOTween` `Cinemachine` `Steamworks.NET`

### 🌀 DIMENSION DROP
**20-player battle royale with dimension-shifting mechanics · Hackathon Project**  
Nov 2026 · Game Client Developer  
2026 Game Talent Institute AI Hackathon

A battle royale where supply drops transform players into characters from different visual dimensions, including pixel art, voxels, and clay. Each dimension has distinct abilities and strengths within a cyclic counter system.

- Implemented the core gameplay loop: movement, shooting, damage handling, weapon and healing-item looting, a shrinking safe zone, and win/loss conditions.
- Set up the main scene and base player, and implemented weapon attachment switching when character models transform.
- Built the HUD and a top-down camera / RenderTexture minimap showing the safe zone, supply drops, and player position.
- Implemented title, spectator, and results screens.
- Built the single-player foundation, then coordinated with a teammate who integrated it into multiplayer.

`Unity 6 (URP)` `C#` `RenderTexture`
